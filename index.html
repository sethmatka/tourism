Worth the journey

Places to Visit

From tiger territory and formidable ramparts to peaceful wetlands, Ranthambhore rewards every kind of curious traveller.

Wildlife

Jungle Safari

Explore Ranthambhore's dry forest, rocky ridges and lakes while looking for tigers, leopards, deer and native birdlife.

Royal Heritage

Amer Fort

Discover grand courtyards, ornate gateways and the glittering Amer Fort at this celebrated hill fort overlooking Maota Lake near Jaipur.

Sacred

Trinetra Ganesh Temple

Seek blessings at the revered three-eyed Ganesh shrine inside Ranthambhore Fort, where devotees also worship Riddhi and Siddhi.

UNESCO Heritage

Ranthambhore Fort

Walk through monumental gateways, ruined palaces and ancient temples at this 10th-century hill fort above the national park.

Sacred

Amareshwar Mahadev Temple

Visit this peaceful Shiva temple set amid the Aravalli hills, a popular local pilgrimage and picnic spot near Sawai Madhopur.

Museum

Rajiv Gandhi Regional Museum

Discover Rajasthan's wildlife, forests and natural history through engaging galleries and educational exhibits in Sawai Madhopur.

Local Culture

Local Handicraft Market

Browse colourful textiles, handmade crafts and locally made souvenirs while experiencing the lively culture of Sawai Madhopur.

Wildlife

Crocodile Sanctuary & Booking

Take a guided excursion to the Chambal region to spot crocodiles, gharials and river birds in their protected natural habitat.

Architecture

Hawa Mahal

Admire Jaipur's iconic pink sandstone facade, whose hundreds of latticed windows once let royal women observe the streets unseen.
